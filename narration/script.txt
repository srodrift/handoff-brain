Quick note: everything you'll see is a realistic example. The clinics and people are made up. Maya is leaving on Friday, and Sam is taking over her five clinics. That's about two and a half hours of calls. Instead of Sam reading all of that, the brain reads every call for him.

And this is where Sam starts. Not a pile of notes. Just what to do first, and why. Lakeview is at the top because Rosa is about to cancel her old provider, and texting still isn't working. If Sam misses that, they're gone.

Open the account, and it's all on one page. The address and contact from Salesforce, and right next to it, what actually happened on the calls. Where they are, what's blocked, and every promise Maya made. The obvious question with AI is: is it making things up? So every quote is checked, word for word, against the real transcript. If Maya didn't say it, it doesn't get the check mark.

Sam can just ask. For what happened, it only answers from the calls. But if he asks what to do, it gives real advice, and it labels that as its own suggestion. So he always knows what's a fact, and what's a recommendation.
