Hi! This is Handoff Brain, built by Sunny, an onboarding consultant. Sunny spends most of the day on calls with customers, and on almost every call, there's a promise. I'll check with the carrier. I'll call your IT person. I'll get back to you Friday. Now imagine Sunny switches teams, or goes on leave. Someone else takes over those customers. They open Salesforce and find a couple of lines of notes. Everything that was actually said is buried in call recordings, and nobody has time to sit through hours of those. So promises slip, and the customer is the one who notices. That's the problem Handoff Brain solves.
